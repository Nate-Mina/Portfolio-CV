Pharmacotherapy Synergy & Combination Protocols

When evaluating Surface Under the Cumulative Ranking Curve (SUCRA) metrics, combining nitric oxide precursor amino acids (L-Arginine, Carnitines) with prescription PDE5 inhibitors yields higher therapeutic efficacy than PDE5 monotherapy alone in organic vascular ED.

Mechanism of Synergy: Amino acid donors expand eNOS substrate availability, elevating baseline cGMP production so PDE5 inhibitors have a greater substrate volume to protect from degradation.

The Online Review Distortion Effect
