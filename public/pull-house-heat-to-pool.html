03. Mathematical Foundation

I. Log Mean Temperature Difference (LMTD)

To calculate the total heat flux Q, we must integrate the temperature difference across the length of the titanium tube. Since the refrigerant and water temps change as they flow, we use the logarithmic average:

ΔTlm =
ΔT1 - ΔT2 ln(ΔT1 / ΔT2)

By using pool water (82°F) vs Air (95°F), we increase ΔT, allowing for a 40% smaller heat exchanger footprint for the same BTU load.
